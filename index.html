Arduino 8-Step Synth V3

Arduino Uno knobs · 20×4 virtual LCD · live browser audio · 8-note sequencer

PIT: +0 CUT: 50\nBPM: 120 LFO: 0

Demo values. Connect Arduino to control the synth.

Sound starts only when you click Start. Turn your third Arduino knob (A3) to change tempo. Start with your computer volume low.

8-step note sequencer

Choose a note for each step, or choose REST for silence. Green indicates playback; orange indicates the note being edited. Editing works during playback: turn the encoder to select a step, press to edit, then turn to change its note. Press again to confirm. Upload the updated Arduino sketch to enable this on the hardware. One step = one eighth note.

A1 = PIT (transpose ±12 semitones) · A2 = CUT (filter) · A3 = BPM (40–240) · A0 = LFO (tremolo). Uses the existing Arduino serial format: pitch,cutoff,thirdKnob,lfo at 115200 baud. Use the matching Step Editor firmware.
